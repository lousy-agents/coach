Can CodeSignal leave pilot?

A reading of the shipped CLI, made by running it. First published 16 Aug 2026. This page is the evidence for leaving pilot. It is not an old snapshot. Closed gaps move to the archive. They are not deleted. The ids stay.

Last reviewed 6 Oct 2026 HEAD 92fabd2 ~190 CLI runs 6 repositories 27th verification pass #282 · 10 / 24 closed

Verdict

Stay in pilot.

The analysis is not the reason. An added file counts as introduced. A rename or a copy is read at the new path. The finding is lifecycle: unknown, and the report carries continuity_not_determined, until old-path continuity exists. What still holds this in pilot is obtain, noise, and a report that is hard to use. The change itself is no longer skipped in silence. Do not sell diff mode as an advisory PR check until a named audience can be told an honest job on a fresh corpus.

Do not widen the audience on this restamp. The Go pilot that already runs can keep using baseline, a layer policy they wrote, and file-local diff mode on adds, modifications, deletes, renames, and copies.
