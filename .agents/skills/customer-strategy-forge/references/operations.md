# Operation Catalog

The numbers preserve a stable vocabulary; they are not a required execution
order. Run only operations selected in the manifest.

## Separate Research Refresh

Never trigger research acquisition silently from synthesis.

### R1. Research Question Designer

Turn a strategic uncertainty into product-independent questions, target
situations, recruitment/source strategy, disconfirming questions, and stopping
criteria. Questions must be capable of challenging the current product idea.

### R2. Evidence Acquirer

Use only human-approved sources/channels and tools. Capture source context,
selection method, date, and permission basis. Founder-authored public posts
remain founder evidence. Product feature lists and automated diagnostics are
not customer needs.

### R3. Evidence Curator

Separate observation from interpretation, deduplicate, preserve
contradictions, document sampling limits, and perform human capture/redaction
review. Publish a new append-only Signal Pack version. Raw PII stays in approved
restricted storage; repository-ready packs contain only approved material.

## Customer Operations

### 1. Inquiry Grounder

Create the Grounding Brief: problem hypothesis, questions, decisions, horizon,
decision date, owner, manifest, constraints, assumptions, selected artifacts,
and exclusions. Do not include product goals before product mapping.

### 2. Evidence Auditor

Apply the evidence audit in
[evidence-and-gates.md](./evidence-and-gates.md). This is the default endpoint
and can be the complete result.

### 3. Actor Ecosystem Mapper

Identify people who perform, receive, review, approve, operate, purchase, bear
risk, or are indirectly affected. Preserve distinct incentives and evidence.

### 4. Scope Selector

Choose a provisional behavioral scope: portfolio, product, workflow, or
situation. Record candidate segments, rationale, exclusions, evidence, and
decision-relevant distinguishing behavior. A persona is optional.

### 5. Product-Blind Signal Synthesizer

Organize triggering situations, desired progress, current behavior and
workarounds, pains, anxieties, constraints, tradeoffs, customer language,
functional/emotional outcomes, evidence of success, contradictions, outliers,
and questions. Do not infer builder capabilities.

### 6. Segment and Contradiction Analyst

Determine whether observations form a coherent behavioral segment. Preserve
minority/high-consequence needs. Investigate whether conflicts arise from
different situations, time, roles, or segments; do not average or split
automatically.

### 7. Needs and Jobs Mapper

Create falsifiable Claim Records through:

```text
evidence
-> observed situation
-> current behavior or workaround
-> obstacle, anxiety, or tradeoff
-> desired progress
-> outcome criterion
```

### 8. Persona and Situation Assembler

Produce only the selected persona and/or situation cards. Exclude unsupported
biography and fields that cannot influence a decision. Qualify every material
behavioral claim and state which decisions the artifact can and cannot inform.

### 9. Progression Context Mapper

Apply an optional versioned progression model only after a version-pinned
behavioral artifact exists for this inquiry. Withhold the model from the
initial synthesizer packet even when this operation is selected; release it
in the progression-release handoff in
[modes-and-routing.md](./modes-and-routing.md). Record observed signals,
scope, range hypothesis, confidence, desired/resisted transitions, blockers,
adjacent states, and evidence. Model interpretations are separate claims.
Never infer progression from title, seniority, or product adoption; do not
assume advancement is uniform, irreversible, or desirable. Do not rewrite
the behavioral baseline to fit the model.

### 10. Journey Synthesizer

Connect situations over time: triggers, actions, decisions, actors, tools,
traveling context/intent/policy/evidence, handoffs, confidence, emotions,
workarounds, abandonment, evidence of progress, and possible next states. A
product-screen or repository-shaped journey fails.

### 11. Independent Critic

Run as the separate Critic role under
[isolation-and-critic.md](./isolation-and-critic.md), not as the synthesizer's
next paragraph.

## Product-Aware Operations

### 12. Portfolio and Opportunity Mapper

After a critic pass, map evidence-qualified jobs, pains, and transitions to
current capabilities, alternatives, partners, and constraints. Record strong
or partial coverage, overlaps, broken handoffs, missing shared artifacts,
unsupported assumptions, intentionally unsupported needs, possible future
modules, and no-action conclusions.

Do not modify passed customer claims. Product mapping that suggests a new
customer claim creates a question for a new product-blind inquiry.

### 13. Experience Coherence and Joy Mapper

Evaluate trust, context-restatement, setup burden, handoffs, relief,
confidence, flow, pride, control, momentum, and pleasant surprise across
relevant moments. Every asserted customer emotion has evidence references and
a claim-level state. Product interventions and delight ideas remain hypotheses.
A run does not need to find joy.

### 14. Strategy and Validation Planner

Record decisions supported now, decisions not supported, high-consequence
assumptions, next research, bounded experiments, success/failure evidence,
portfolio changes to consider, and Decision Log entries. “No product action”
is a valid decision.
